Manage your Agent tasks right from your ME Terminal!
Place orders just like in vanilla AE2, track progress, and switch CPUs (Agents)!

[Download JAR](https://github.com/TGFT-0/Applied-Intelligence/releases/download/v0.11.3-alpha/applied-intelligence-0.11.3-alpha-mc1.21.1-neoforge.jar) · [User guide](docs/USAGE.en.md)

Use Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.169, and AE2 19.2.19.

Run Codex CLI, DeepSeek Harness, Pi, and OpenCode 2 locally in private singleplayer worlds.
